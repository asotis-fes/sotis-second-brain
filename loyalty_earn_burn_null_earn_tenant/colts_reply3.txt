To answer Ben directly: no, bonus is not always redeemed before base. There's no "bonus first" rule in the matcher — it's purely oldest-expiration-first (then oldest creation). So the only way the Colts bonus gets burned first is if it actually expires sooner than the person's other FanCash.

And that's where the problem is: this earn did NOT come through with a 1-day expiry. In both fanflow (the source) and the ledger, Daniel's Colts bonus was created 8/13 and expires 8/13/2027 — 365 days out. So even though it was configured as 1-day, the actual FanCash record has a 1-year expiry. That's why FIFO passed it over — his base FC (an older earn expiring 4/2027) was the soonest-expiring, so it got burned first.

So Rohan's logic is exactly right — IF it were 1-day expiry it should've been burned first. The issue is the 1-day isn't landing on the earn. Two possibilities worth checking on the issuance side:
- The 1-day might be the code/claim window (how long they have to claim the code), which is separate from how long the FanCash lasts once claimed.
- Or the FanCash expiry is genuinely supposed to be 1 day and issuance isn't applying it — because what we see on the actual event is 365 days.

Bottom line: if the claimed FanCash actually expires in ~1 day, FIFO will burn it first and this tracks cleanly with no matcher change. Right now it's showing a 1-year expiry, which is why it didn't. Can someone on the issuance/config side confirm what expiry is being set on the FanCash itself (not the code)?
